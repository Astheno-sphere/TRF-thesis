# Appendix D. Correspondence with Public Authorities

*Reproduced so that the one figure in this thesis that rests on correspondence, the six-year retention period of the Norwegian cross-border pilot, can be checked by the reader. ## D.1 Helsedirektoratet, on the retention of the cross-border audit trail

**Enquiry.** Sent by the author on Monday 5 October 2026, 15:48, to Helsedirektoratet's central mailbox (postmottak). It asked for the source and duration of the audit-repository retention requirement for the Norwegian National Contact Point for eHealth (NCPeH) and its MyHealth@EU cross-border audit trail.

**Reply.** Received Monday 19 October 2026 from Hans Löwe Larsen, Avdelingsdirektør (Director of Department), Division of Information Management and Method, Helsedirektoratet. Verbatim:

> Hei.
> Takk for din henvendelse, og det er bare hyggelig å kunne være til assistanse.
>
> 1. Jeg finner ingen spesifikk henvisning til lengden på oppbevaring i requirements catalouge eller tilhørende dokumentasjon (Security Services Specification, System Architecture Specifications, NCPeH Architecure Specifications, Audit Trail Profil osv). Det henvises til nasjonal lovgivning, men konkretiserer ikke lagringsmåte eller lagringstid.
>
> 2. Under utprøvingen er det besluttet en lagringstid på 6 år, men en endelig beslutning når det går i full produksjon er ikke tatt. Hdir er dataansvarlig og vil ta den beslutningen.
>
> Håper dette besvarer dine spørsmål, bare ta kontakt dersom du har behov for ytterligere informasjon.
> Med vennlig hilsen
> Hans Löwe Larsen

**Translation (the author's).**

> Hello. Thank you for your enquiry; it is a pleasure to be of assistance.
>
> 1. I find no specific reference to the length of retention in the requirements catalogue or the associated documentation (Security Services Specification, System Architecture Specifications, NCPeH Architecture Specifications, Audit Trail Profile, etc.). Reference is made to national legislation, but neither the manner nor the period of storage is specified.
>
> 2. During the pilot, a retention period of 6 years has been decided, but a final decision for when it goes into full production has not been taken. Hdir is the data controller and will take that decision.
>
> I hope this answers your questions; do get in touch if you need further information.
> Kind regards, Hans Löwe Larsen

**What the thesis takes from it.** The directorate finds no retention period in the eHDSI requirements catalogue or the specifications it names; they refer the matter to national law; for the Norwegian pilot the directorate, as controller, has decided on six years, with the production figure undecided and reserved to Helsedirektoratet as data controller (Chapter 2, Section 2.3). The thesis therefore treats the cross-border floor as a parameter (Chapter 4, Section 4.3) and uses ten years only as an illustrative long value.

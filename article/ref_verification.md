## Body questions

Article 68(12) EHDS: yes. Official text (OJ copy read) says the data in the secure processing environment "shall be deleted ... within six months of the expiry of the data permit". Same paragraph sets permit duration (reported as up to 10 years, extendable once; that detail not cross-checked). Read in the sanidad.gob.es copy, not EUR-Lex HTML; paragraph number as reported consistently in two fetches.

Recitals on deletion or retention of logs in SPEs: none found. I scanned recitals 1-115 in three chunks. No recital speaks to log retention or to deletion after permit expiry in an SPE. Closest: recitals 16, 19, 36, 39 (logging for access services and EHR systems, not SPEs); recital 77 (only non-personal data may be downloaded from an SPE); recital 68 (audits in HDAB activity reports). I cannot give recital numbers for SPE log retention because none was found. Caveat: the scan was done by a summarising fetch tool and recitals 1-67 were checked in a first chunk that ended mid-recital 68, so a missed recital is possible. Re-check against EUR-Lex HTML.
